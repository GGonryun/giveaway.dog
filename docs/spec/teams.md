# Teams

What a team keeps to itself, what each role in a team may do, and how people join a team.

## Isolation

- `TEAM-001` A person who is not a member of a team (a host of another team, a participant or a visitor) cannot open the team's pages, and the response holds none of the team's data.
- `TEAM-002` A host cannot see another team's giveaways, entrants or winners through the pages of their own team.
- `TEAM-003` A team sees a user, in its Users list and on the user's profile, only when that user entered one of the team's giveaways.
- `TEAM-004` The entries of a user that a team sees are only the entries in that team's giveaways.
- `TEAM-005` A host cannot change the records of another team, such as its X pickers or the draws of its prizes.
- `TEAM-006` A visitor or a person who is not a member of a team cannot run the team's host actions.

## Roles

- `ROLE-001` A blocked member cannot open the team's pages or run its host actions.
- `ROLE-002` A member or a guest cannot change the team profile, the social links or the members of the team.
- `ROLE-003` An admin can manage the invite link and remove members, but cannot change a member's role or the team name.
- `ROLE-004` No one can remove the owner of a team.
- `ROLE-005` An admin cannot make anyone an owner, through an invite or otherwise.
- `ROLE-006` A guest cannot connect or disconnect the integrations of the team.
- `ROLE-007` A guest or a blocked member cannot complete a giveaway of the team.
- `ROLE-008` A guest cannot draw the winners of a giveaway of the team.

## Invites

- `INVITE-001` A person invited by email joins the team, with the role of the invite, through the link in the email.
- `INVITE-002` An expired invite link is refused: its page is not found, and it adds no one to the team.
